Encrypt a ticket

Turns a ticket PDF into an encrypted tickets/<name>.enc that the app opens after you unlock codes. Everything happens in this page — the PDF and passphrase never leave your device. This page holds no tickets or codes, so it is safe to publish.

1 — Ticket name

2 — The ticket PDF

3 — Family passphrase

Checked against the published secrets.enc.js first, so a typo can't produce a ticket the app won't open.

Afterwards: send the downloaded .enc file to Claude, or put it in the tickets/ folder and push. Only ciphertext gets published.
